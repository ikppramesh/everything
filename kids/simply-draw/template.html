Magic Effects

5 Ways to Bring Your Drawing to Life

Shading is what makes drawings look realistic — it adds depth, dimension, and turns flat sketches into something magical. Pick your style and watch the transformation.

🏺
Clay
3D bump mapping makes your drawing look sculpted in warm terracotta clay, with real directional lighting and shadows.
🎨
Watercolor
Pastel washes of color fill your shapes, with a subtle paper texture that gives it a hand-painted feel.
⚡
Neon
Your lines glow with rainbow neon light on a deep dark background — like your drawing came to life in a city at night.
✏️
Pencil Sketch
Cross-hatching and shading added automatically on warm cream paper, just like a professional artist's sketchbook.
🌈
Rainbow
Every region of your drawing gets a different rainbow hue with 3D shading — colorful, vibrant, and joyful.
