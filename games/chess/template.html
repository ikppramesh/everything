Features

What's Included

♟️
Complete Rule Engine
Every chess rule implemented: piece movement, captures, check detection, and illegal move filtering.
🏰
Castling
Both king-side and queen-side castling with full validity checks — no castling through or out of check.
👻
En Passant
The tricky en passant pawn capture is fully supported and highlighted as a valid move when available.
👑
Pawn Promotion
When a pawn reaches the last rank, a dialog prompts for Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight promotion.
💡
Move Hints
Click any piece to see valid destinations: dots for empty squares, rings for capturable pieces.
📋
Move History
All moves logged in algebraic-style notation in a scrollable history panel.
