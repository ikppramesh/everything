What's Included

🤖
Unbeatable AI
The computer uses the minimax algorithm, always playing optimally. The best you can do is a draw.
👥
Two Player Mode
Pass the device and play head-to-head. Mode is selectable from the start screen each session.
🏆
Score Tracking
Wins, draws, and losses are tallied across rounds until you switch modes or refresh.
✨
Win Highlighting
Winning cells glow orange so the decisive line is immediately obvious after every game.
🎉
Result Overlay
A clean win/draw card appears after each match with options to replay or switch game mode.
📱
Fully Responsive
Works great on phones, tablets, and desktops — the board scales to fit any screen size.
