Snake

The timeless arcade classic — eat, grow, survive. Built with HTML5 Canvas with smooth animations, speed scaling, and full mobile touch support.

  • Canvas-rendered 20×20 grid with animated snake
  • Speed increases every 5 points (level up)
  • Persistent high score via localStorage
  • Arrow keys, WASD, and D-pad (mobile)
  • Swipe gestures for touchscreen play
  • Pause / resume with Space bar
  • Death flash animation & game over screen

Features

What's Included

🐍
Smooth Canvas Rendering
Drawn frame-by-frame on an HTML5 canvas with rounded cells, gradient snake body, and expressive head eyes.
⚡
Adaptive Speed
Every 5 food eaten bumps the level and tightens the tick interval, ramping challenge from 220ms down to 80ms.
🏆
High Score Persistence
Best score is saved to localStorage and survives page refreshes. A "New High Score!" banner appears on beats.
📱
Touch & Swipe Controls
Full swipe gesture detection plus an on-screen D-pad — works great on phones and tablets without a keyboard.
⏸️
Pause Support
Hit Space at any time to pause and resume without losing state. A pause overlay makes the status clear.
💥
Death Animation
On collision, the snake flashes red before the game-over screen appears — giving visual impact to the final moment.
