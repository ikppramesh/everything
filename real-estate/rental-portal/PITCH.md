# Rental Portal — Business Pitch

---

## Executive Summary

The **Rental Portal** is an enterprise-grade digital solution for Property Owners, Tenant, Real Estate Brokers. It eliminates manual, paper-based processes and replaces them with a unified, real-time management platform — delivering measurable ROI within the first quarter of deployment.

---

## Problem Statement

Rental agreements are paper-based, maintenance requests go untracked, and rent payments are unorganized.

**Current Pain Points:**
- Manual data entry leading to errors and duplication
- No real-time visibility into operations and KPIs
- Disconnected tools (WhatsApp, Excel, paper registers)
- Compliance gaps and audit trail missing
- High dependency on individual staff knowledge

**Market Context:**
India has over 1 million businesses in the Real Estate sector. Less than 15% have adopted purpose-built software. The remaining 85% rely on legacy tools, creating a massive digitization opportunity.

---

## Our Solution

A comprehensive, cloud-ready platform that manages listings, applications, agreements, maintenance — all from a single dashboard.

**What makes it different:**
- Purpose-built for Property Owners, Tenant, Real Estate Brokers
- Works offline and online
- No training required — intuitive UI
- Configurable workflows
- Real-time notifications and alerts
