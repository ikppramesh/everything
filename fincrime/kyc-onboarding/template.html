STEP BY STEP

Six screens. One complete workflow.

The simulator walks through every stage of a real KYC/CDD onboarding process — from collecting personal details to issuing a risk-rated decision.

1
Personal Details Name, DOB (18+ validated), nationality with country risk badge, ID type with regex validation, residential address
2
Document Verification Simulated upload zones for ID and address proof. 3-second animated verification — auto-verified or flagged for manual review
3
PEP & Sanctions Screening Five name entries across OFAC/EU/UN/Interpol. Analyst actions per match. Notes required if match detected
4
Source of Funds Six income categories with conditional fields. Inline red-flag indicators for high-risk sectors like crypto and cash-intensive
5
CDD / EDD Risk Assessment Five risk factor cards with animated SVG gauge. Composite score drives Standard / Enhanced / EDD / Decline decision
6
Risk Rating & Approval Full summary table, approve with auto-generated Customer ID, structured decline form, collapsible audit trail
