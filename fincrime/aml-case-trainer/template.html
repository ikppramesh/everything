All 8 cases at a glance

Each case spans a distinct sector and typology, from ₹4.2 crore structuring schemes to ₹24 crore sanctions evasion.

CASH STRUCTURING • RETAIL
The Nail Bar Network
12 Mumbai nail salons depositing ₹4.8-4.9 lakhs daily — just below the CTR threshold. Revenue 3-7x industry norms.
REAL ESTATE ML • PROPERTY
Project Sunrise
14 luxury apartments sold with BVI shell company buyers, no purchase agreements, and director fund extraction.
TBML • TRADE FINANCE
The Ghost Exporter
Engineering components invoiced at 4.7-5.6x market value to a related-party HK buyer. LC-funded over-invoicing.
VIRTUAL ASSETS • CRYPTO
Crypto Casino
Mixer-tainted BTC deposits, 5% wagering, withdrawals to clean wallets. Classic casino wash-through layering.
DNFBP • LEGAL SERVICES
The Helpful Solicitor
₹8.4 crore through a law firm trust account. Rapid pass-throughs, BVI funds, no CDD. Lawyer claims privilege exemption.
SANCTIONS • CORPORATE FINANCE
Shell Company Maze
UK → BVI → Cayman → Luxembourg → sanctioned Russian national. ₹24 crore processed. OFAC SDN match flagged.
PEP CORRUPTION • GOVERNMENT
The Friendly Politician
Minister's wife receives ₹50 lakhs/year from state government contract winners. Classic kickback channel.
TBML / HAWALA • TEXTILE
Fast Fashion Fraud
Garments under-invoiced 30-45% to UAE buyer; shortfall returned via hawala. Integration through cash deposits.
