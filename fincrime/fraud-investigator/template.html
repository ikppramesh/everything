The Overnight Account Takeover

CASE 02
The Phantom Card
Card-Not-Present Fraud

How the Investigation Works

A structured three-stage process mirroring real fraud case management.

🎭
3 Case Files

Account Takeover, Card-Not-Present fraud, and Synthetic Identity bust-out. Each case represents a distinct fraud typology with unique evidence patterns, attack vectors, and regulatory responses. Difficulty ranges from Medium to Hard.

🔍
Evidence Review

24 evidence items across four categories — Digital (IP logs, device fingerprints), Financial (transaction history, amount patterns), Customer (statements, KYC), and External (telecom checks, credit bureau, industry intelligence). A dynamic case timeline builds as you open each item.

🌲
Decision Trees

Each case presents 3-4 scored analytical questions reflecting real investigator decisions: Is this fraud? What attack vector? What regulatory action is required? Every choice reveals a consequence — correct or incorrect — explaining the professional reasoning. Scores are benchmarked against the model analyst.

⚖️
Verdict System

Each case resolves with a verdict badge — Fraud Confirmed, Genuine, or Inconclusive — with a confidence percentage, fraud type classification, and a prioritised action list covering STR filings, chargeback procedures, RBI zero-liability compensation, and cross-bank coordination. Best scores are saved locally.
