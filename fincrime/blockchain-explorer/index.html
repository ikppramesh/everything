Why Can't You Tamper With Blockchain?

  1. Every block contains the hash of the previous block.
  2. Changing any data changes that block's hash.
  3. The next block's prevHash no longer matches.
  4. You must re-mine that block AND every block after it.
  5. On Bitcoin, this means redoing years of work — before the honest chain grows further.

This is why blockchain is tamper-evident.
