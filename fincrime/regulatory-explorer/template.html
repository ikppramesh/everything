How It Works

Three Panels, One Workflow

The tool is structured around three panels designed to support both learning and day-to-day reference work.

1

Navigate

Use the left sidebar to browse frameworks and sections. Search filters the tree in real time.

2

Read

The center panel loads a detailed reference card with the obligation, penalty, enforcement body, and related regulations.

3

Apply

The right panel presents scenarios. Pick a scenario, test your judgment, then reveal the full analysis with applicable laws and deadlines.

4

Reference

Use the FATF watch list panel to quickly check the jurisdiction risk status of any country involved in a transaction.
