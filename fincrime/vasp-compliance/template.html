Core Modules

Four tools. One compliance workflow.

Each tab in the simulator covers a distinct compliance use-case, from on-chain forensics to regulatory reporting guidance.

🔗
Transaction Graph

Visualise an eight-node blockchain transaction flow rendered on HTML5 Canvas. Nodes are risk-coloured (low / medium / high / critical) with pulsing borders for sanctioned addresses. Flagged edges are drawn in red with directional arrowheads. Click any transaction edge to inspect full details — amount, timestamp, risk flags, and recommended compliance action — in the side panel.

Travel Rule Checker

Run FATF Recommendation 16 compliance checks on any virtual asset transfer. A built-in registry covers 15 VASPs across India, USA, EU, and offshore jurisdictions. Checks include: amount threshold, VASP registration status, unhosted wallet detection, originator completeness, and live sanctions screening. Returns a PROCEED / CAUTION / REJECT verdict with per-check details.

Wallet Screener

Score any wallet address on a 0–100 risk scale using five exposure categories: Exchange, Mixer, Darknet, Sanctions, and Gambling. The visual gauge updates instantly with colour-coded levels. Five pre-loaded sample wallets demonstrate a full range from clean to OFAC-sanctioned. Each result includes last-5 transactions and a tailored recommended action card.

How It Works

A four-step compliance screening workflow, from transaction receipt to reporting decision.

1

Receive Transfer

A virtual asset transfer arrives at your VASP from an originator.

2

Screen the Wallets

Run originator and beneficiary addresses through the Wallet Screener for risk scores and sanctions checks.

3

Check Travel Rule

Verify that all required FATF R.16 information is present and that both VASPs are registered.

4

Act on Verdict

Proceed, apply EDD, or reject and file an STR based on the tool's compliance verdict.

Built on FATF Standards

All compliance logic follows the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) Recommendations for Virtual Assets and Virtual Asset Service Providers, updated guidance notes from 2019–2023, and relevant national implementations including India's FIU-IND framework.

FATF R.15 FATF R.16 PMLA India OFAC SDN FIU-IND MiCA (EU)
  • R.15 VASPs must be regulated for AML/CFT purposes and subject to adequate supervision or monitoring by a competent authority.
  • R.16 The Travel Rule requires VASPs to obtain, hold, and transmit required originator and beneficiary information for VA transfers of USD/EUR 1,000 or above.
  • Unhosted Transfers to/from unhosted (self-hosted) wallets require enhanced due diligence. VASPs should verify the ownership of the counterparty wallet.
  • Sunrise The "Sunrise Problem" arises because jurisdictions implement the Travel Rule at different times. VASPs must apply a risk-based approach to non-compliant counterparties.
