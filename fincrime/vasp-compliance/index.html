FATF VASP Guidance

Key regulatory obligations for VASPs
R.15
VASPs must be regulated for AML/CFT purposes and subject to adequate supervision or monitoring.
R.16
Travel Rule: VASPs must obtain, hold, and transmit required originator and beneficiary information for VA transfers of USD/EUR 1,000 or above.
Unhosted
Transfers to/from unhosted wallets require enhanced due diligence. Identity verification of the self-hosted wallet owner is recommended.
Sunrise
Not all jurisdictions have implemented the Travel Rule simultaneously. VASPs must manage the "Sunrise Problem" by applying a risk-based approach to non-compliant counterparties.
